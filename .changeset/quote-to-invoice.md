Turn a sent or accepted quote into a draft invoice in one click. "Create invoice" on the quote page copies the client, currency, tax rate, notes, and line items (hourly items become estimated hours at the hourly rate, complimentary and included items are listed at no charge, and royalty items are left off for later) and opens the new invoice for editing. The quote is marked converted and links to its invoice, and the invoice shows which quote it came from. Deleting that invoice returns the quote to accepted so it can be invoiced again.
